# Clothing Image Style

Use this prompt when generating replacement clothing images for the dressing app.

## Base Prompt (Reima-Inspired Photorealistic Style)

Professional product photography of a single high-quality children's outdoor clothing item, Nordic design aesthetic, functional sportswear style, clean modern look, realistic technical fabric texture, vibrant practical colors (bright blues, teals, oranges, reds, yellows), professional studio lighting, pure white background, item laid flat or on invisible form, sharp commercial photography, centered composition, soft natural shadows, no person, no mannequin, no hanger, no brand logos, no text, no labels, no visible tags.

Style reference: Nordic children's outdoor wear, technical sportswear, durable playful design, weather-resistant fabrics, reflective details, practical zippers and closures, rounded toddler-friendly shapes.

Colors to prefer: Bright teal, ocean blue, coral orange, sunshine yellow, berry red, forest green, purple, soft pastels for base layers.

## Consistency Notes

- Generate one item per image in photorealistic product photography style
- Keep the item large, centered, and easy for a 3-year-old to recognize
- Use the same camera angle across all items: front-facing, laid flat or on invisible form
- Show realistic fabric texture: fleece texture on fleece items, smooth shell fabric on jackets, knit texture on hats
- Include functional details: zippers, velcro closures, elastic cuffs, reflective strips (no brand logos)
- Use vibrant Nordic outdoor colors: teals, bright blues, oranges, yellows, reds
- Maintain professional commercial product photography quality
- Pure white background, soft natural shadows
- Square canvas, 1024x1024 px or larger for high quality

## Negative Prompt

child wearing clothes, model, person, mannequin, hanger, store display, brand logo, text, watermark, labels, tags visible, busy background, multiple clothing items, dark gloomy colors, harsh shadows, wrinkled messy fabric, clutter, emoji, sticker, toy-like, flat illustration, cartoon style, thick outline, low quality, blurry, amateur photography
